# 0004 · Where messages go

**Decided:** 28 Sep 2026, by Marcos.

Every system and developer alert (urgent pages included), the 08:00 digest
and the briefs go to the Telegram group "AdHunters alerts". Pushcut only
delivers end-user watches (an operator's every move, Raposa investigation
events, later verticals rising). People talk on WhatsApp.

**Why:** developer noise stays out of the phones of people who only care about
what they chose to watch.
